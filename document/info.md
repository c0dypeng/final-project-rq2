# RQ2: NLA Prediction Stability

**Hypothesis:** NLA's stability in describing activation(s) reflects the target model's uncertainty.

---

## Implementations

| # | Approach |
|---|----------|
| 1 | Check **semantic similarity** of NLA predictions across tokens |
| 2 | Check **average log p** (i.e., perplexity) of NLA's output |
| 3 | Check **stability** of NLA predictions for a single token |
